**Answer-first:** `tanhdev.com` collects non-personally identifiable log files automatically for system security, performance tuning, and traffic analytics.

Like standard technical websites, `tanhdev.com` collects system log data generated by web servers and edge networks. Information stored in hosting log files includes:

## 3. Google Analytics 4 & Consent Mode v2

**Answer-first:** `tanhdev.com` uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) with Consent Mode v2 enabled to measure site traffic while respecting visitor privacy choices.

### Consent Signals & States
We implement GA4 Consent Mode v2 to ensure measurement tags adjust dynamically based on user consent:
- `analytics_storage`: Controls cookie storage for analytics modeling and performance measurement.
- `ad_storage`: Controls cookie storage for advertising purposes.
- `ad_user_data`: Controls whether user data can be sent to Google for online advertising purposes.
- `ad_personalization`: Controls whether personalized advertising (remarketing) is enabled.

When consent is withheld, GA4 operates in a cookieless mode, transmitting un-itemized, functional pings to model traffic without reading or writing tracking cookies on visitor devices.

## 6. User Data Protection Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

**Answer-first:** We fully support visitor privacy rights under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

### European Union (GDPR) Data Rights
Every user in the European Economic Area (EEA) is entitled to the following rights:
- **Right to Access:** Request copies of your personal data held by us.
- **Right to Rectification:** Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** Request deletion of your personal data under certain conditions.
- **Right to Restrict Processing:** Request restriction of processing under specified circumstances.
- **Right to Object to Processing:** Object to our processing of your personal data.
- **Right to Data Portability:** Request transfer of collected data to another organization or directly to you.

### California Resident (CCPA/CPRA) Rights
California consumers have the right to:
- **Right to Know:** Request disclosure of categories and specific pieces of personal information collected.
- **Right to Delete:** Request deletion of personal information collected from the consumer.
- **Right to Opt-Out of Sale/Sharing:** `tanhdev.com` **does NOT sell or share** personal information to third parties for monetary or valuable consideration.
- **Right to Non-Discrimination:** Receive equal service and pricing regardless of exercising privacy rights.

### How to Exercise Your Rights
To exercise any of these privacy rights, contact us via email at **vesviet@gmail.com** with the subject line *"Data Privacy Request"*. We respond to all verified requests within **30 calendar days**.
