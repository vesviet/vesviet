> **Answer-first:** The FinTech category covers the architecture of high-assurance financial platforms, double-entry accounting ledgers, distributed Saga workflows, and ISO 20022 messaging standards. Applying these financial engineering blueprints ensures absolute transactional immutability, mathematically eliminates double-spending anomalies, and sustains audit-ready reconciliation across high-velocity payment processors handling millions of daily banking operations.

## Core Technical Focus Areas

- **Double-Entry Accounting & Ledger Immutability:** Mathematical balance enforcement ($\sum \text{Debits} \equiv \sum \text{Credits}$), event-sourced ledger models, and audit logs.
- **Distributed Financial Sagas:** Orchestrated saga execution, compensating transactions, idempotency keys, and multi-service account isolation.
- **Regulatory & Messaging Standards:** BIAN (Banking Industry Architecture Network) service domains, ISO 20022 financial messages, and audit trail retention.
