## 5. Architectural Invariants & Governance Rules

1. **One-Way Authority Topology**: `learn.tanhdev.com` is the Vietnamese educational playbook that channels all external authority UPWARD to `tanhdev.com` via header blockquote banners. `tanhdev.com` serves as the canonical English engineering authority and maintains zero backward links to `learn.tanhdev.com`.
2. **Self-Referential Canonical Routing**: Every radar post and monthly archive specifies its exact absolute URL as `canonicalURL`, preventing duplicate content penalties across cross-domain syndication.
3. **Answer-First Standard**: Blockquotes immediately under `# H1` must be strictly <= 60 words, opening with `> **Answer-First:**` to provide instant decision intelligence for executive architects and AI search crawlers.
4. **Permanent Edge Redirects**: All legacy URLs, dated aliases, and migrated slugs are registered in `static/_redirects` with 301 status, verified via `test_redirects_oracle.py`.
