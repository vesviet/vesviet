## AI Content Governance (2025-2026)
- **AI-Assisted Content Policy**: All content must strictly follow a **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL)** governance model. AI tools (e.g., LLM outline generators, AST code checkers) are permitted solely for initial research summarization, code linting, or transcript formatting. Autonomous publishing of AI-generated content is strictly prohibited.
- **Human Editorial Review Gate**: Mandatory for 100% of published pieces. Every article must pass human editorial verification for technical accuracy, code compilation, and brand voice alignment before deployment.
- **SME Review Lock**: All high-stakes technical, financial, and core banking articles (YMYL / high-stakes backend architecture) must receive sign-off from a designated Subject Matter Expert.
- **Information Gain Requirement**: Each published post must contain at least one exclusive element — a custom benchmark, runnable production code snippet, custom Mermaid sequence flow, or proprietary local market data — that cannot be synthesized by standard LLMs.
- **AI Citation & GEO/AEO Monitoring**: Monthly tracking of Vesviet citations across Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, ChatGPT, and Claude to assess Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) effectiveness.
